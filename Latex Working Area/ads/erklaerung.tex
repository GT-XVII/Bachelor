\section*{Declaration on the Use of AI Tools}

\vspace*{1em}

I used ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude / Claude Code (Anthropic) as supporting tools during the preparation of this thesis. I recall the model designations as ``5.6 Luna'' for ChatGPT and ``Opus'' for Claude; I did not retain exact model versions or dates of individual interactions and cannot verify them retrospectively.

For software development, I planned the purpose and structure of each script and used both tools for agentic pair programming, including code generation, implementation, debugging, and iterative correction until the scripts worked. This assistance extended across the acquisition, retrieval, agent, and evaluation code. I reviewed and tested the resulting implementations and take responsibility for the design decisions and reported results.

For the written thesis, I produced the initial drafts. I used Claude across the chapters to check spelling and sentence structure and to correct LaTeX formatting errors. Its role in writing was to polish my drafts. I created all diagrams without generative AI assistance. I reviewed the suggested revisions and take responsibility for the final text, code, and figures.

I used the tools through the IDE terminal and did not retain conversation transcripts or individual development and editing prompts from these terminal sessions. I therefore cannot supply a complete interaction log or an exact prompt-by-prompt allocation to chapters or files retrospectively. This declaration records my recollection of a generally consistent workflow across the chapters and code. Appendix~\ref{app:materials} identifies the available code and experimental prompt templates and distinguishes them from the unavailable assistance history.
